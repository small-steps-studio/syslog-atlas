# Pro reports

* **Show Sources**: Hosts and programs with their line counts and errors, CEF devices and the top CEF signatures.
* **Show Security Events**: Failed and accepted logins, sudo and su, high-severity CEF events, and possible brute force sources.

The first Pro command starts a free 14-day trial on this computer. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase: USD 19 (Personal) or USD 39 per user (Business).
